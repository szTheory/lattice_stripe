<objective>
Prove that `stream!/4` paginates correctly — and, more importantly, that it does not silently leak across
tenants or silently change what it is measuring when it crosses a page boundary.

Purpose: stripe-mock cannot prove any of this. It returns one synthetic item and ignores both page size
and cursor, so a green integration suite says nothing about pagination. Mox at the transport is the only
place these nine facts are observable. Two of them are tenancy boundaries, and the stakes here differ from
the entitlements phase in a way worth stating precisely: because all three filters are required, a total
drop makes Stripe 400 loudly, but a partial drop leaks undetectably — the returned summaries carry no
customer field to compare against, so nothing downstream can notice.

Output: one focused test file carrying the nine assertions, two of them mutation-checked.
</objective>
