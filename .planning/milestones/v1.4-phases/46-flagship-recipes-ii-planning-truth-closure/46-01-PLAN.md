<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| onboarding redirects and bearer URLs -> durable account truth | Redirect handlers and link URLs are UX transport, not durable authority |
| destination-charge request success -> platform payment truth | A successful create call can be mistaken for payout or reconciliation truth before webhook follow-through |
| quote acceptance -> downstream billing truth | A successful quote acceptance can be mistaken for invoice or subscription settlement even though the proof is bounded |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-46-01 | I | Connect onboarding and login links | mitigate | Keep `AccountLink` and `LoginLink` URLs framed as bearer credentials and redirect immediately without logging or persistence |
| T-46-02 | T | Connect money-movement guidance | mitigate | Teach destination charges as the default one-seller path and keep webhook-owned truth explicit for payment, fee, and payout follow-through |
| T-46-03 | R | Quote downstream follow-through wording | mitigate | Keep the proof boundary short and explicit: accepted transition now, authoritative downstream truth later from follow-up reads and webhooks |
</threat_model>
